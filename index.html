THE AQUACULTURE CHALLENGE

A Health Challenge
Beneath the Surface

01

Disease Pressure

Shrimp aquaculture faces significant disease challenges, including WSSV and bacterial diseases associated with pathogenic Vibrio spp.

02

Farm Losses

Disease can contribute to mortality, reduced growth and poor farm economics.

03

Antimicrobial Pressure

Dependence on antimicrobial interventions creates concerns around resistance, residues and responsible aquaculture.

04

Need for Prevention

Farmers need practical, scalable and evidence-driven approaches that support prevention and overall shrimp health.

02
OUR SOLUTION

Prevention Starts With
Nutrition

A phytobiotic-fortified functional feed concept designed to incorporate selected plant-derived bioactives into shrimp nutrition.

PhytoFortified is under development and to be validated through controlled and field trials. It is not presented as a proven therapeutic treatment or a guarantee of disease prevention.
